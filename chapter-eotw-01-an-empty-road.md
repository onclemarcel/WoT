# EotW Ch. 1 — An Empty Road

Oct 12, 2026 · @OncleMarcel

Up: Wheel of Time — Story Map · Threads: WoT Thread — Perrin · World: WoT — World Index

Chapter 1 of *The Eye of the World* plants the whole series in miniature: a quiet farm road, a winter that won't end, and one rider only Rand can see. It follows the prologue "Dragonmount", where Lews Therin kills his family and himself in the Age of Legends, and the drop from that to a cart of cider on a muddy road is the series' scale in two scenes. The chapter is told entirely from Rand's point of view.

## Setting

The setting is calm on the surface and wrong underneath: spring should have come, and it hasn't.

| Place | What it shows | What it hides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Quarry Road | Rand and Tam take a cart of brandy and cider from their Westwood farm to Emond's Field for Bel Tine; mud, stone walls, bare woods | An empty road where something is watching them |
| The weather | Late winter: no buds, a cold wind, farmers worried for the crops | The Dark One's touch on the world, not yet named |
| Emond's Field | The green being dressed for the festival; cheerful, gossipy, stubborn villagers | A small world that hears of the outside only as rumour |
| The Winespring Inn | The village's heart, where Bran al'Vere is innkeeper and mayor | Old foundations and an older past the village has forgotten (see WoT — Manetheren) |

## Characters

Only Rand and Tam carry the chapter; the village is a chorus, and most of the main cast has not arrived yet.

| Character | Mindset | Wants | Key moment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rand al'Thor | Young, unworldly, mostly content; then uneasy and doubting his own senses | An ordinary life, the festival's fun; his thoughts keep drifting to Egwene | Sees a black-cloaked rider whose cloak doesn't move in the wind. Its gaze brings cold fear and something like hatred; when he looks again it is gone, and Tam saw nothing |
| Tam al'Thor | Steady, practical, calm; travels with a spear while Rand carries a bow | To get the cart to the village and the festival done well | Neither dismisses Rand nor panics: the composure Rand will lose and spend books trying to find again. His past outside the Two Rivers is only hinted at later |
| Bran al'Vere and the villagers | Busy with the festival and grumbling about the weather (Cenn Buie among them) | Normal life, a good Bel Tine, maybe a gleeman or a peddler | Gossip about a false Dragon somewhere outside the Two Rivers, and about Aes Sedai, brings the wider world in as rumour |

**Not yet on stage.** Mat and Perrin first appear in Chapter 2, "Strangers", where it turns out they saw the rider too; Moiraine and Lan arrive in the same chapter. Perrin's first scene is therefore seen through Rand's eyes, not his own.

## Craft lessons

1. **Calm first.** Chores, weather and festival gossip take their time, so the rider lands as a violation of that calm.
2. **One witness.** Only Rand sees the rider, which gives him doubt and isolation and gives the reader a mystery.
3. **The world as rumour.** Big events arrive as gossip, keeping the focus tight while hinting at the scale beyond.
4. **Setting as a sign.** The winter that won't end carries the threat before any character names it.
5. **Contrast with the prologue.** Cutting from the end of an age to a farm cart sets the stakes without explaining them.

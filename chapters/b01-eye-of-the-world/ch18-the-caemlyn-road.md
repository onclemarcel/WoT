Chapter 18 is the party's first battle, and it ends at the gate of a dead city. Three days east of Baerlon, Trolloc horns sound behind them; Lan rides back and counts perhaps five hundred, then horns answer from ahead too. Moiraine turns north off the road. On a hillside they charge through a Trolloc line while Lan beheads a Fade, and the boys shout war cries without knowing why. Three more Fades come; Moiraine stops them with an earthquake and a wall of fire, and nearly falls from the effort. Moiraine explains Mat's cry: the war cry of Manetheren, and the old blood still sings. With the horns closing again, Lan leads them to the one place the Trollocs will not go: Shadar Logoth.

## The countdown and the war cries

The chapter builds its tension as a countdown in Rand's head, then releases it in a charge where three Two Rivers boys shout what they did not know they knew.

**The countdown.** Each horn shortens the distance Rand guesses.

1. A horn from the west: "ten miles, no more". Lan rides back alone.
2. Horns call and answer: "Eight miles. Maybe seven."
3. "Five miles", and Lan returns: three to five fists of Trollocs, perhaps five hundred, on them within the hour.
4. Horns answer from ahead as well. The party is being driven; Moiraine turns north.
5. In the hills: "Two miles. Maybe less." Scouts are near.
6. Over a hilltop: the Trolloc line itself, a Fade at its center.
7. After the fight: three Fades on the next hill, a hundred spans away.

**The war cries.** Lan's cry sets the others off, and each one reaches for a different past.

| Who | Cry | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lan | "For the Seven Towers!" | His own lost land; Moiraine calls him "last Lord of the Seven Towers" |
| Rand, then Perrin | "Manetheren!" | Moiraine's tale at Winternight (Ch. 9); Perrin: "That gives us a right" |
| Mat | "Carai an Caldazar! Carai an Ellisande! Al Ellisande!" | He does not know; it is "all foggy". Moiraine: the war cry of Manetheren and of its last king, "for the honor of the Red Eagle" and "the Rose of the Sun" |
| Egwene | (none) | For a moment she understood Mat, then lost it |

**What the old blood does to the friendship.** Moiraine says "the old blood still sings" in Arad's line. Rand and Perrin both think, and are ashamed to think, that the Trollocs may be after Mat alone.

The chapter's two loudest clues are a name for Lan and a cry from Mat, and neither man explains himself.

## Reader's notes

Your notes read the chapter as a turning point in two relationships and a lesson in what a Fade is.

| Note | Reading |

## Craft lessons

1. **Measure tension in numbers.** Rand's guessed distances (ten miles, seven, five, two) let the reader feel the trap close without a word of description.
2. **Spring a second trap.** Horns from ahead turn a chase into an encirclement. The reader learns what Moiraine learns at the same moment.
3. **Write a first battle as a blur.** Rand cannot remember "any particular part". The muddle and the heat in the cold wind say more than a clear choreography would.
4. **Let a character speak what he does not know.** Mat's cry in a dead tongue tells the reader that the past lives in him, and frightens him.
5. **Make magic cost.** The earthquake and the fire wall end with Moiraine swaying in her saddle, and the next spell, a small false trail, takes even more.
6. **Give the escape a price.** The only refuge is one the Aes Sedai wanted to avoid. The chapter ends on a name, not an explanation.

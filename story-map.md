## Macro shape

The series gathers its cast, splits it, lets the threads sprawl, then converges them. Jordan wrote Books 1–11 and *New Spring*; Sanderson wrote Books 12–14 from Jordan's notes.

1. **Gathering (Books 1–3).** A Tolkien-style quest that keeps the group together. It ends with Rand claiming the Dragon title in Tear.
2. **Expansion (Books 4–6).** The characters split into separate POV threads. Rand's power grows, peaking at Dumai's Wells.
3. **Sprawl (Books 7–11).** Many threads advance slowly in parallel, the so-called slog. It is a case study in braided threads that stop converging.
4. **Convergence (Books 12–14).** Threads close one after another and feed into Tarmon Gai'don.

## How the map grows

Each level is its own doc, linked from its parent, so no page gets cluttered.

1. **Hub (this doc).** Threads and macro shape.
2. **Thread satellite.** The key events of one thread in book order, and how its characters change along it.
3. **Arc or event satellite.** One turning point, such as Dumai's Wells: setup, payoff, and which threads cross there.
4. **Book or chapter satellite.** How one book or chapter interleaves its POVs and where its climax lands.
5. **Scene and paragraph study.** A close read of how a single scene or passage is built, studied and paraphrased rather than reproduced.

One cross-cutting satellite is worth considering later: a character index that links each major character to the threads they move through.

# WoT Character — Nynaeve

Oct 12, 2026 · @OncleMarcel

Up: [Wheel of Time — Story Map](../story-map.md) · Chapter cards: [EotW Ch. 1 — An Empty Road](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch01-an-empty-road.md) · [EotW Ch. 2 — Strangers](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch02-strangers.md) · [EotW Ch. 3 — The Peddler](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch03-the-peddler.md)

Nynaeve's arc is about a healer whose strength is her anger and whose weakness is the same anger. The young Wisdom who scolds the whole village becomes one of the most powerful Aes Sedai alive, but only after she learns to use her power without waiting to be furious. This card is a first version; it will deepen as the chapter cards advance.

## Starting point

Chapters 1 to 3 build Nynaeve's reputation before she appears, then show it in a single scene.

- **Who she is:** the Wisdom of Emond's Field, unusually young for it, a healer with herbs and a stick she uses on fools of any age.
- **What others think:** Council men grumble she's too young; she won't tell them what she hears on the wind.
- **What she wants:** to protect her people, and to be obeyed while she does it.
- **What she fears, unspoken:** being thought a child, as Moiraine calls her; losing control.
- **Her signature gesture:** walking straight into the Council's closed meeting, sure the Women's Circle will ask the right questions.
- **Her pupil:** Egwene, whom she is teaching to listen to the wind.
- **What Moiraine lets slip (Chapter 13):** untaught women who can channel often become Wisdoms (Ch. 12), and there are "two in one village" in Emond's Field. Moiraine takes it back at once: "Her road lies another way, I fear." The text names no one.

**Chapter 15: she follows.** Off stage, Nynaeve reaches Baerlon alone. The ferry is sunk, so she roots Master Hightower out of his bedroom and bullies him into rowing her and her horse across the Taren himself. At the Stag and Lion she is closeted with Moiraine, "cold enough in there to snow". The boys face her "as if to face Trollocs".

## Senses and surroundings

Nynaeve's signature sense is a healer's: she reads bodies, herbs and weather, and her temper is the switch on her power.

### Inner senses

- **Listening to the wind.** The Wisdom's gift to sense coming weather, which is really the One Power used unknowingly.
- **The block.** For years she can channel only when angry; she has to work herself into a rage to heal or fight.
- **Healing.** She feels illness as something to be cut out, and later finds ways to heal what others think beyond help.
- **The braid.** Tugging it is her tell for anger; the reader learns to watch it.

## Craft lessons

1. **Tie a power to an emotion.** The anger block makes every use of her gift a scene about her temper.
2. **Give a strong character a visible tell.** The braid-tug lets the reader read her before she speaks.
3. **Reverse the hierarchy.** The teacher who becomes her pupil's subordinate creates lasting friction.
4. **Make growth a surrender.** Her breakthrough comes when she stops fighting and accepts help.

# WoT Character — Mat

Up: [Wheel of Time — Story Map](../story-map.md) · Chapter cards: [EotW Ch. 1 — An Empty Road](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch01-an-empty-road.md) · [EotW Ch. 2 — Strangers](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch02-strangers.md) · [EotW Ch. 3 — The Peddler](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch03-the-peddler.md)

Mat's arc is about a man who runs from responsibility and keeps being handed more of it. The prankster who wants only fun becomes a general with the memories of dead commanders, the husband of an empress, and the man who wins the Last Battle's war. This card is a first version; it will deepen as the chapter cards advance.

## Starting point

Chapters 1 to 3 give Mat complete on arrival, which makes the later change measurable.

- **Who he is:** Rand's friend, the village prankster who "never seemed to grow up"; floured dogs, ghost hounds, a badger loosed on the Green.
- **How he talks:** "Burn me", "Blood and ashes"; quick, mocking, never serious for long.
- **What he wants:** fun, novelty, a good story: the gleeman, the fireworks, a look at the false Dragon.
- **What he fears:** the rider scared him more than anything ever had, and he admits it.
- **His instincts:** reckless (he goaded Bili Congar into naming the Dark One), yet sound when it counts (he is the one who decides Moiraine's coin must not be spent).
- **His tell:** curiosity stronger than caution, the trait that will take him into the most dangerous places in the series.

## Arc

The arc runs from poisoned boy (Books 1–2) to reluctant general (3–6) to a man who stops running from what he is (7–14). The Why column carries the lesson.

| Book | Turning point | What shifts | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Takes the ruby dagger from Shadar Logoth | It poisons him with suspicion and slowly kills him | His greed and curiosity open the door to the old city's evil |
| 2 | Healed in Tar Valon; blows the Horn of Valere at Falme | Bound to the Horn and to the story | Luck and impulse make him the one who sounds it |
| 4 | Steps through the redstone doorway; hanged on Avendesora and saved by Rand | Gains old soldiers' memories, the medallion and the spear | The price of answers is a life he never asked for |
| 5–6 | Founds the Band of the Red Hand | The prankster becomes a commander | The memories make him good at what he swore he didn't want |
| 7–11 | Ebou Dar; meets and kidnaps Tuon, the Seanchan heir; marries her | Bound to the empire he distrusts | His own prophecy, and his own heart, catch up with him |
| 13 | Enters the Tower of Ghenjei to rescue Moiraine; loses an eye | Keeps his oath to Thom and Moiraine | Loyalty wins over self-preservation |
| 14 | Commands the armies of the Light; kills Padan Fain | The gambler is trusted with everything | He finally plays for the highest stake on purpose |

## Senses and surroundings

Mat's signature sense is luck: he feels it as dice tumbling in his head, which stop when something important is about to happen.

### Inner senses

- **The dice.** A warning that fate is about to land; when they stop, the moment has come.
- **Luck.** Strongest when chance is pure, which makes him a gambler before he is a soldier.
- **Other men's memories.** Battles he never fought, in languages he never learned, surfacing when he needs them and when he doesn't.
- **The medallion.** Cold against his chest; the One Power cannot touch him while he wears it.

## Craft lessons

1. **A comic voice can carry the gravest arc.** Mat's jokes make his growth feel earned, not preached.
2. **Give luck a body.** The dice in his head make an abstract power physical and turn it into a tension device.
3. **Reluctance is a renewable engine.** Each time Mat runs, the story hands him a bigger duty, and each acceptance matters.
4. **Pay off the first book in the last.** The dagger from Shadar Logoth links Mat and Fain across all fourteen books.

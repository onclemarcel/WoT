# WoT Character — Lan

Oct 12, 2026 · @OncleMarcel

Up: [Wheel of Time — Story Map](../story-map.md) · First appears: [EotW Ch. 2 — Strangers](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch02-strangers.md) · Bonded to: [WoT Character — Moiraine](moiraine.md)

Lan's arc is about a man who has given his life to a lost war learning to accept that he may live, and love, and lead. The uncrowned king of a dead nation becomes the general who rides out to lead his people again at the Last Battle. This card is a first version; it will deepen as the chapter cards advance.

## Starting point

He arrives in Chapter 2 almost without words, defined by how he moves and how he looks at people.

- **What the village sees:** a tall swordsman in a cloak that seems to fade into whatever lies behind it; hair long and grey at the temples, held by a leather band; a face of "stony planes and angles."
- **How he moves:** Rand "could think of nothing but a wolf."
- **How he looks:** cold blue eyes that weigh the three youths and give nothing away.
- **What Ewin guesses:** "a Warder"; Mat laughs it off; Ewin is right.
- **What he hides:** he is al'Lan Mandragoran, uncrowned king of Malkier, a Borderland swallowed by the Blight, and Moiraine's Warder.

## Arc

| Book | Turning point | What shifts | Why |

## Senses and surroundings

Lan's signature sense is the bond: he feels Moiraine's presence and condition at all times, and its loss nearly destroys him.

### Inner senses

- **The Warder's bond.** He knows where his Aes Sedai is and how she feels; she can lend him strength.
- **Stillness.** The swordsman's calm, close to Tam's void, that Rand learns from him.
- **The grief of Malkier.** A loss he carries silently, as duty rather than sorrow.

### Places

| Place | Feeling | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Malkier | A memory he never had | Swallowed by the Blight when he was a baby |
| The Borderlands | Home in all but name | Where the war against the Shadow never stops |
| The Blight | His destiny | Where he expects to die, and where he rides at the end |
| The road with Moiraine | Duty | Years of guarding her search |

### Objects

- **The fancloth cloak.** Fades into whatever lies behind it: Chapter 2's sign of a Warder.
- **His sword.** A blade forged with the Power, and the heron-mark of a master swordsman.
- **The hadori.** The braided leather band of a Malkieri man.
- **The golden crane.** Malkier's banner, which others raise for him.

## Craft lessons

1. **Introduce a character by motion and gaze.** Lan barely speaks in Chapter 2; how he moves and looks is enough.
2. **A man devoted to death is a great love interest.** His refusal of Nynaeve creates conflict that is noble on both sides.
3. **Let others choose to follow.** His return to kingship works because the Malkieri come to him unasked.
4. **Tie a mentor to the hero's tools.** Lan teaches Rand the sword and the calm under fire.

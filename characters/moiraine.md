# WoT Character — Moiraine

Oct 12, 2026 · @OncleMarcel

Up: [Wheel of Time — Story Map](../story-map.md) · First appears: [EotW Ch. 2 — Strangers](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch02-strangers.md)

Moiraine's arc is about control and surrender. She spends twenty years searching for the Dragon Reborn and steering him, then has to learn that he will only win if she lets him choose, and finally gives up her own life to save his. This card is a first version; it will deepen as the chapter cards advance.

## Starting point

She arrives in Chapter 2 as "the Lady Moiraine", seen entirely from outside.

- **What the village sees:** an ageless, graceful lady from a gleeman's tale, in blue velvet and gold, with a serpent ring and a blue stone on her brow.
- **What she does:** asks everyone's age and how long their families have lived here; calls Nynaeve "child"; gives Rand, Mat and Perrin each a silver coin as a "token" of a bond.
- **What she says:** "a student of history" interested in what happened here long ago; men wear "many names, many faces."
- **What she hides:** she is an Aes Sedai of the Blue Ajah, and she has come for the Dragon Reborn, not knowing yet which boy he is.
- **How she speaks:** every line true and every line misleading; see Chapter 2's "read twice" table.

## Senses and surroundings

Moiraine's signature trait is composure: the reader almost never sees inside her in the early books, so her calm becomes a mystery to read.

### How she is perceived

- **Ageless face.** Young at first glance, older the longer anyone looks: the mark of an Aes Sedai, unexplained at first.
- **Presence.** Small, but "her height seemed the proper one"; others feel clumsy beside her.
- **Silence.** She reveals by what she withholds; her smile fades only once, when Rand asks why she came.

### Places

| Place | Feeling | Why |

### Objects

- **The serpent ring.** The Great Serpent, mark of every Aes Sedai.
- **The blue stone on her brow.** A detail in Chapter 2 that turns out to have a use of its own.
- **The coins.** Tokens that tie the boys to her.

## Peoples and powers

| Group or power | Relationship over time | Effect on her |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aes Sedai (Blue Ajah) | Her order, which she partly works around | Gives her power, and the oaths that shape how she speaks |
| Cairhienin nobility | Her birth, which she rarely mentions | A habit of intrigue and command |
| Warders | Lan, her Warder and friend | A bond she gives up to protect him |
| The Eelfinn | Captors | The price of her bargain for Rand's sake |
| The Forsaken | Enemies who know her as a threat | She removes Lanfear at the cost of herself |

## Craft lessons

1. **Keep the mentor opaque.** Never showing her thoughts early makes her both trustworthy and suspect.
2. **Bind speech with a rule.** Aes Sedai cannot lie, so her every evasion becomes a puzzle for the reader.
3. **Remove the mentor to free the hero.** Her disappearance forces Rand to lead alone.
4. **Bring the mentor back changed.** Her return completes her arc instead of undoing it.

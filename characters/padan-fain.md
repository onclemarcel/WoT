# WoT Character — Padan Fain

Oct 12, 2026 · @OncleMarcel

Up: [Wheel of Time — Story Map](../story-map.md) · Thread: [WoT Thread — The Shadow](../threads/shadow.md) · First appears: [EotW Ch. 3 — The Peddler](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch03-the-peddler.md)

Padan Fain is the villain hidden in plain sight: the cheerful peddler every Two Rivers child knows, already a Darkfriend when the story opens. He starts as the Shadow's tool and becomes something worse, a hatred of his own that serves neither the Light nor the Dark One.

## Starting point

In Chapter 3 he is the familiar spring peddler: pale, skinny, big-nosed, "always smiling and laughing as if he knew a joke that no one else knew." His friendliness is "of a peculiarly distant kind, backslapping without ever getting close."

- **What the village sees:** a showman who loves an audience and sells news as well as pins.
- **What he hides:** he is a Darkfriend, and the Dark One has made him a hound on the trail of the young men from the Two Rivers. The Trollocs and Fades follow where he leads.
- **His tell:** the "sharp, searching look" he gives Rand when Rand's shout breaks the silence, and his amusement at news that frightens everyone else.
- **What he wants:** at first, to serve; later, Rand himself, out of a hatred that becomes his whole being.

## Arc

Fain's arc is a descent in three steps: servant of the Shadow, a man fused with an older evil, and finally a hatred with no master.

| Book | Turning point | What shifts | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The peddler in Emond's Field; later revealed as the Dark One's hound, and caught | From comic villager to Darkfriend | He was twisted at Shayol Ghul to track the boys, and his trail brings the Trollocs |
| 1 | In Shadar Logoth, the evil of the dead city and its counsellor Mordeth enters him | He is no longer only the Dark One's | The city's taint answers his own hatred and merges with it |
| 2 | Escapes Fal Dara with the Horn of Valere and the Shadar Logoth dagger, killing as he goes | He sets his own trap for Rand | He wants Rand, not the Shadow's victory |
| 4 | Returns to the Two Rivers under another name, with the Whitecloaks and Trollocs | He wounds the homes and families of the heroes | Revenge on the place and people tied to Rand |
| 7 | Strikes Rand with the Shadar Logoth dagger | Rand carries a second wound that will not heal | The dagger spreads the old city's poison |
| 14 | Becomes something like the mist of Shadar Logoth and enters the Last Battle | He is beyond both Light and Shadow | Hatred has consumed the man; Mat, whom the dagger once poisoned, kills him |

## Senses and surroundings

Fain is never a point-of-view character for long; the reader knows him through other people's senses, and every sense reports something wrong.

### How others perceive him

- **Sight.** The beak of a nose, the pale skinny frame, the grin that is always half a joke at someone's expense.
- **Smell.** To Perrin's nose Fain smells of corruption, even when he looks harmless.
- **Manner.** Theatrical, then sly, then openly mad: his voice shifts as Mordeth's evil takes hold.

### Places

| Place | What it means for him |
| --- | --- |
| The Two Rivers | His yearly round as a trusted peddler; later the place he returns to hurt |
| Shayol Ghul | Where the Dark One remade him into a hound |
| Shadar Logoth | The dead city whose evil enters him; his true home after that |
| Fal Dara | His prison, which he escapes with the Horn and the dagger |

### Objects

- **The wagon.** His cover: goods, news and a stage.
- **The Shadar Logoth dagger.** A ruby-hilted blade carrying the city's poison; it nearly destroys Mat and leaves Rand a wound that never heals.

## Peoples and powers

| Group or power | Relationship over time | Effect on him |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Darkfriends and the Dark One | Servant, then rebel | Gives him his first purpose, then a master to despise |
| Shadar Logoth (Mashadar, Mordeth) | Absorbed into him | An evil older than and separate from the Shadow, hostile to both sides |
| Trollocs and Myrddraal | Hunters who follow his trail, later forces he commands | Proof that he has moved from tool to leader of his own war |
| Whitecloaks | A cover he hides behind in the Two Rivers | Lets him turn righteous men into instruments of his hatred |

## Craft lessons

1. **Hide the villain in the familiar.** The yearly peddler everyone trusts is the one who led the hunters; the betrayal hurts because the reader liked him first.
2. **Leave a tell on the page.** The searching look and the private joke are fair warnings a second reading catches.
3. **A third force breaks the two-sided war.** Fain hates both the Light and the Shadow, so he can strike anyone, anywhere, and the plot never settles into two camps.
4. **Let the villain change.** Servant, possessed man, then pure hatred: each stage makes him less human and more dangerous.
5. **Tie characters together with a cursed object.** The dagger links Fain, Mat and Rand, and makes Mat's final blow a closing of accounts.

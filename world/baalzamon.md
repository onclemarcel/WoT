# WoT — Ba'alzamon

Up: [WoT — World Index](index.md) · Thread: [WoT Thread — The Shadow](../threads/shadow.md) · Chapter cards: [EotW Ch. 9 — Tellings of the Wheel](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch09-tellings-of-the-wheel.md) · [EotW Ch. 14 — The Stag and Lion](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch14-the-stag-and-lion.md) · Dreamer: [WoT Character — Rand](../characters/rand.md)

Ba'alzamon is the face the Shadow shows Rand in his dreams, and in Book 1 Rand takes him for the Dark One himself. He first appears unnamed in Chapter 9, as a figure in a cloak "the colour of dried blood" with a face like embers. In Chapter 14 he names himself, "Some call me Ba'alzamon", and talks.

- **How he looks:** a handsome man in dark, well-cut clothes, in the prime of life; for an instant at a time his mouth and eyes open on endless caverns of flame.
- **Where he waits:** a stone room at the end of a corridor of identical doors, with a balcony on a sky of streaming red and black cloud, a fire with no heat, and a mirror that cannot show Rand.
- **What he wants:** to know if Rand is "the one", and Rand's consent: to drink, to doubt the Tower, to serve. "Serve me" (Ch. 9); "You will serve me" (Ch. 14).
- **His weapons:** temptation (the wine), insult (Rand's parents as the Tower's breeding stock), history (every disaster his work), fear of the Tower, and pain: he breaks a rat's back with a crooked finger, then bends Rand the same way.
- **What he claims:** that he was never bound in Shayol Ghul; that he drove Lews Therin to kill his family, sent the Trollocs south, and turned Artur Hawkwing against Aes Sedai; that "the dead belong to me".
- **What Rand answers:** a cup put down untasted, "Don't you talk about them!", and the catechism: "You are bound in Shayol Ghul".

## Ba'alzamon across the series

The name is a mask, and the series removes it slowly. From memory, to be confirmed as the books arrive.

| Book | Where Ba'alzamon stands | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Visits all three boys' dreams; faces Rand at the Eye of the World and seems destroyed | The reader learns the Shadow's voice before its armies |
| 2 | Returns in dreams; duels Rand in the sky over Falme and wounds his side | A defeat that does not hold; the wound stays with Rand |
| 3 | Killed by Rand in the Heart of the Stone; revealed as Ishamael, first of the Forsaken, not the Dark One | The mask comes off: the tempter was a servant claiming his master's name |
| Middle books | Returns in a new body as Moridin, the Nae'blis, and shares a strange link with Rand | The enemy becomes Rand's dark mirror |
| 14 | Meets his end at Shayol Ghul | The long argument with the Dragon ends where it began, in Rand's mind |

The name itself is said to mean "Heart of the Dark" in the Old Tongue, which is why the boys take it for the Dark One.

**Craft lesson.** Give the villain a voice early, and let him lie with true facts. Ba'alzamon's history matches the tales the reader knows; only his part in it is his claim, and that is what makes it hard to dismiss.

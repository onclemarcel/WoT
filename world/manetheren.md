# WoT — Manetheren

Oct 12, 2026 · @OncleMarcel

Up: [WoT — World Index](index.md)

Manetheren is the dead kingdom under the Two Rivers. Its memory turns farm boys into heirs of a legend and gives the villagers a pride to rise to.

## History

- **A great nation.** Manetheren was one of the Ten Nations that rose after the Breaking. It was known for its courage and its loyalty to the fight against the Shadow.
- **The last stand.** During the Trolloc Wars, King Aemon's army held against a Shadowspawn horde, on the land that is now the Two Rivers.
- **The fall.** When Aemon died, Queen Eldrene drew so much of the Power in grief that she destroyed herself, the city and the enemy.
- **Forgetting.** Over the centuries the villagers forgot what they descend from, and the Two Rivers became a quiet backwater nominally under Andor.

## Echoes

- **Moiraine's story.** In Book 1 she tells the villagers who their ancestors were. It is the first time the Two Rivers is pushed to see itself as more than farms.
- **The red eagle banner.** It is raised again when the Two Rivers defends itself in Book 4. A symbol turns a militia into a people.
- **The Old Tongue.** Mat recalls Manetheren's battle cries through borrowed memories, so the past lives on in a person.
- **Stubbornness.** The Two Rivers folk are famously stubborn, which the story presents as an inherited trait of a people who would not yield.

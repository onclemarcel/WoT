# WoT — Mentions Index

Up: [Wheel of Time — Story Map](../story-map.md) · [WoT — World Index](index.md)

This index lists every term the chapter cards name, so a satellite card can be built later from what the chapters show. It is a repo-only file, not a Docs card. The rules for adding rows and for turning a term into a card are in [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md), under "Satellite cards".

Chapters are Book 1 (*The Eye of the World*) chapter numbers. Each entry says in a few words how the term appears there. "Index row" means the World Index has a row for it but no card yet.

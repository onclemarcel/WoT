# WoT — Tar Valon and the White Tower

Oct 12, 2026 · @OncleMarcel

Up: [WoT — World Index](index.md) · Chapter cards: [EotW Ch. 3 — The Peddler](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch03-the-peddler.md) · Aes Sedai on stage: [WoT Character — Moiraine](../characters/moiraine.md)

Tar Valon is the island city of the Aes Sedai, and the White Tower at its heart is their seat. Chapter 3 names it for the first time: "A party of them has ridden south from Tar Valon", says the peddler, and the village learns that the women of the stories are part of the present.

- **Where:** an island in the River Erinin, in the shadow of Dragonmount.
- **Who:** women who channel the One Power, organised in seven Ajahs and led by the Amyrlin Seat.
- **The bind:** each Aes Sedai swears the Three Oaths, among them never to speak a word that is not true.
- **Its men:** Warders, bonded swordsmen; Tar Valon also hunts and gentles men who channel.

## Rumour against truth

In Chapter 3 the boys argue about Aes Sedai with nothing but stories to go on, and the Children of the Light are named in the same breath, as a second power people are afraid of.

| Who says it | What they say | What is true |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rand | "The Dragon may have started it, but it was Aes Sedai who actually broke the world" | Half true: male Aes Sedai, maddened by the taint on saidin, broke it; the women's half held what it could |
| Mat, quoting the stories | Some stories say Aes Sedai serve the Dark One | A few do: the Black Ajah exists, though the Tower denies it |
| Bili Congar | Aes Sedai and Darkfriends don't exist; they are just stories | Both exist, and both are in Emond's Field within days |
| The guard, via Mat | Many believe the Dragon will save them, but are afraid to say so, "afraid of the Aes Sedai or the Children of the Light" | Both orders hunt false Dragons; the Children also hunt Aes Sedai, whom they call witches |
| Cenn Buie | Some things must not be spoken of | Fear of the Power is the default in the west |

The Children of the Light enter as a name only. They are a military order, the Whitecloaks, who see Darkfriends everywhere and count all Aes Sedai among them; Perrin's story crosses theirs in Book 1.

## Tar Valon across the series

The Tower starts as a single, feared power and spends the series splitting and healing, which is the White Tower thread on the Story Map.

| Books | What happens | Who is touched |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Moiraine's party rides south; the Amyrlin Siuan Sanche backs her secret search | Moiraine, the boys |
| 2–4 | Egwene and Nynaeve train in the Tower; the Black Ajah flees after murders and theft | Egwene, Nynaeve, Elayne |
| 5 | Siuan is deposed and stilled; Elaida takes the Amyrlin Seat; the Tower splits | Siuan, Elaida, Egwene |
| 6–10 | Rebel Aes Sedai raise Egwene as Amyrlin; their army besieges Tar Valon | Egwene |
| 12 | The Tower reunites under Egwene after the Seanchan raid | Egwene |
| 14 | The Aes Sedai fight together at the Last Battle | All |

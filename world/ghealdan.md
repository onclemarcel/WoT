# WoT — Ghealdan

Oct 12, 2026 · @OncleMarcel

Up: [WoT — World Index](index.md) · Chapter cards: [EotW Ch. 3 — The Peddler](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch03-the-peddler.md)

Ghealdan is the first foreign nation the reader hears of: in Chapter 3 the peddler says there is war there, because a man calling himself the Dragon Reborn has raised his standard and can channel. It is a small kingdom east of the Mountains of Mist, so the war is distant for the Two Rivers but not unreachable.

- **Chapter 3's news:** war in Ghealdan; the snows of the Dhallin Forest are red; armies and great houses march against the false Dragon.
- **The false Dragon:** named Logain in Chapter 14 (Logain Ablar in later books). He is one of the rare false Dragons who can truly channel, which is why his rising frightens the whole west.
- **Why it reaches the village:** Fain's news is the first proof that the outside world is at war, a word the Two Rivers has not had to use.
- **Chapter 13's news, at Baerlon's gate:** a big battle in Ghealdan, started when Aes Sedai arrived; some or all of them may be dead. The false Dragon can use the One Power, has "thousands following him", calls them the People of the Dragon, and is said to be moving east and south toward Tear. The Whitecloaks use him as their reason to be in Baerlon. In Chapter 14 Lan confirms the battle at the Stag and Lion and names the victor, Logain; whether the Aes Sedai were killed, spared or went over to him, the stories disagree. That night Ba'alzamon names Logain too, as a man the Amyrlin is using.

## Ghealdan across the series

Ghealdan keeps being the place where a false prophet takes hold, first a false Dragon, then a fanatic preaching the real one.

| Books | What happens in Ghealdan | Who is touched |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Logain's rising and defeat; he is taken to Tar Valon, paraded through Caemlyn on the way | Rand sees him in a cage |
| 2 | Logain is gentled, cut off from the Power | The White Tower |
| 5 | Masema, the Prophet of the Dragon, terrorises the country; Nynaeve and Elayne pass through Samara | Nynaeve, Elayne, Galad |
| 7–8 | Queen Alliandre swears to Perrin, seeking protection from the Prophet | Perrin, Faile |
| 9–11 | Alliandre is taken captive with Faile by the Shaido | Perrin, Faile |

**The yin and yang of false Dragons.** Logain's rising is one of several signs that the Dragon's return is near: the Pattern throws up false Dragons, and the Dark One's stirring shows in the weather and the wolves. Both sides of the struggle are moving at once.

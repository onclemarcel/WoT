# WoT — Emond's Field Villagers

Oct 16, 2026 · @OncleMarcel

Up: [WoT — World Index](index.md) · Home: [WoT — The Two Rivers](two-rivers.md) · Chapter cards: [EotW Ch. 1 — An Empty Road](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch01-an-empty-road.md) · [EotW Ch. 2 — Strangers](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch02-strangers.md) · [EotW Ch. 3 — The Peddler](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch03-the-peddler.md) · [EotW Ch. 4 — The Gleeman](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch04-the-gleeman.md)

The villagers of Emond's Field are the story's chorus: the ordinary people the heroes come from and must protect. In the first chapters they are mostly comic, quarrelling over omens and pranks, but each is drawn with one fixed trait, so the reader knows them by the time the danger arrives.

- **Three who matter most.** Bran al'Vere, the Mayor (Ch. 1, 2, 4); Cenn Buie, the doom-saying thatcher (Ch. 1, 3, 4, 6); Haral Luhhan, the blacksmith (Ch. 2, 3, 5, 6).
- **How they govern.** The Village Council of men, led by the Mayor, and the Women's Circle, led by the Wisdom; see the Two Rivers card.
- **How they meet the outside.** With half-remembered stories, closed doors and stubbornness: they argue over Fain's news and close the inn to the crowd (Ch. 3).
- **Why the author needs them.** A chorus makes the village real, so that when it is threatened, the reader feels what could be lost.

## The villagers across the series

The chorus grows up with the heroes. Comic in Book 1, they become fighters when the Shadow comes back for their home, and their stubbornness turns out to be Manetheren's courage.

| Book | Where the villagers stand | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Festival preparations, gossip and omens; on Winternight Trollocs burn half the village, and the villagers rally to rebuild (Ch. 7) | The life the heroes leave, and the first cost of the Shadow's hunt |
| 4 | Perrin comes home to a land under attack; the villagers fight Trollocs and Whitecloaks, and raise Manetheren's banner | The chorus becomes an army; Perrin becomes their lord |
| 5–13 | The Two Rivers grows, with refugees, and sends men to follow Perrin | Home changes while the heroes are away |
| 14 | Two Rivers men fight in the Last Battle | The farmers of the first chapter stand at the end of the world |

**The lesson.** The same trait that is comic in Chapter 1 is heroic in Book 4. Cenn's contrariness and Bran's patience are the stubbornness that holds a wall.

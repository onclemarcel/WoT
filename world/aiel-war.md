# WoT — The Aiel War

Oct 16, 2026 · @OncleMarcel

Up: [WoT — World Index](index.md) · People: [WoT — Aiel](aiel.md) · Veteran: [WoT Character — Tam](../characters/tam.md) · Chapter card: [EotW Ch. 6 — The Westwood](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch06-the-westwood.md)

The Aiel War is the war Tam fought, and the war Rand was born in. The first six chapters never name it. It reaches the reader only through Tam's fever in Chapter 6, in pieces and out of order, and the pieces fit together into one story.

- **The cause.** The Aiel, who "make no peace with strangers", gave the King of Cairhien a sapling of Avendesora, the Tree of Life, as a sign of peace. It grew for a hundred years. Laman cut it down: "Blood was the price for Avendoraldera."
- **The invasion.** The Aiel "came over the Dragonwall like a flood". The nations called them savages and lost battle after battle before they "stood together against them".
- **The battles Tam names.** The field at Marath, "carpeted with the dead"; the towers of Cairhien burning "like torches"; the Shining Walls, where the Aiel were finally "turned back".
- **The end.** On "the slope of the mountain", in the snow, Tam found a baby whose mother, one of the Aiel women who fight, had given birth alone and died. He and Kari named him Rand.
- **When.** About twenty years before the story, from memory: Rand's age dates it.

## The war across the series

The war ended before the story began, but it keeps returning: as a memory, as a place, and as a pattern the later books repeat. Each return tells a little more of what Tam's fever only hints at.

| Book | How the war returns | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *New Spring* | The prequel is set at the war's end, at the Shining Walls: Moiraine and Lan meet, and a Foretelling announces the Dragon's birth on Dragonmount | The day Tam found Rand is the day the search for him began |
| 1 | Tam's fever (Ch. 6); its veterans, Tam and Lan, carry it in their skills | The war is the hidden past of the people guarding Rand |
| 2 | Rand reaches Cairhien, still scarred by the war and ruled by intrigue | The fever's burning city becomes a real place |
| 4 | In Rhuidean Rand learns who his birth parents were, and the Aiel learn their own history | The baby in the snow gets a name and a people |
| 5 | The Aiel cross the Dragonwall again, this time behind Rand, and fight for Cairhien | The invasion repeats, with the foundling at its head |

**Why it matters.** The war makes Rand both outsider and insider: born to the invaders, raised by a soldier who fought them.

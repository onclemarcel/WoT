# WoT — Shadar Logoth

Up: [WoT — World Index](index.md) · Chapter cards: [EotW Ch. 18 — The Caemlyn Road](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch18-the-caemlyn-road.md) · [EotW Ch. 19 — Shadow's Waiting](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch19-shadows-waiting.md) · Ally of: [WoT — Manetheren](manetheren.md)

Shadar Logoth is a dead city that destroyed itself, and its evil is not the Dark One's. Once it was Aridhol, one of the Ten Nations and an ally of Manetheren. Under the counsellor Mordeth it fought the Shadow with the Shadow's own weapons, suspicion and hate, until those bred Mashadar and nothing living was left. Trollocs and Fades remember, and will not go in. That makes it a refuge, and a trap.

- **Where:** in the forest north of the Caemlyn Road, between Baerlon and the Arinelle, in the wilderness east of the Hills of Absher.
- **What it looks like:** vine-covered walls taller than the trees, guard towers of uneven height, a gate fifty men wide; inside, marble palaces with domes of every shape, colonnades, dry bronze fountains and broken statues; ten or fifty times the size of Baerlon.
- **Its names:** Aridhol, then Shadar Logoth, "the Place Where the Shadow Waits", or Shadow's Waiting.
- **Mashadar:** something born of the city's hate, "locked in the bedrock", still hungering.
- **Mordeth:** the counsellor who poisoned King Balwen's mind; snared, not consumed, by Mashadar. He casts no shadow, swells and thins like smoke, and offers treasure.
- **The rules:** its gifts "twist the mind and taint the spirit"; if Mordeth leads someone to the walls he takes that body and leaves; its creatures cannot stand the sun; an Aes Sedai's wards keep them out.
- **What the Shadow learned here:** a whole army of Trollocs, Darkfriends, Myrddraal and Dreadlords camped in the ruins and vanished, leaving blood and pleas to the Dark One on the walls.

## Shadar Logoth in Book 1

The city enters as a rumour of safety and turns, within one chapter, into the most dangerous place the party has slept.

| Chapter | What the text shows | What it does |

| 19 | The empty city; Moiraine's wards around a white stone shell; Mordeth and his treasure; Mat's dagger; watchers at dusk; the tale of Balwen, Mordeth, Caar and Mashadar; over a hundred Trollocs driven inside by Fades | The refuge becomes a trap from two sides |

## Across the series

The city stays behind, but what it gives away travels. This table is from memory and kept thin.

| Book | Where Shadar Logoth stands | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The party crosses it by night and is scattered; Mat carries the dagger out; Padan Fain, following them, is changed by Mordeth there | One night spreads the city's taint into the story |
| 2 | Mat sickens from the dagger and is Healed in Tar Valon, but not cut free of it | A gift that "waxes and wanes until it rules or kills" |
| 3–8 | Fain carries the city's evil in himself, enemy of the Shadow and the Light alike | A third evil in a two-sided war |
| 9 | Rand uses the city in the Cleansing of saidin: the taint and Mashadar destroy each other, and the city with them | The two evils cancel out |
| 14 | Mat kills Fain at the Last Battle with the same dagger | The dagger closes its own story |

**Craft lesson.** Give the world an evil that belongs to neither side. Shadar Logoth shows the war's moral danger in a place: a city that became what it fought.

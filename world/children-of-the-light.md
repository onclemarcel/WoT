# WoT — Children of the Light

Oct 12, 2026 · @OncleMarcel

Up: [WoT — World Index](index.md) · Chapter cards: [EotW Ch. 3 — The Peddler](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch03-the-peddler.md) · [EotW Ch. 15 — Strangers and Friends](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch15-strangers-and-friends.md) · Rivals: [WoT — Tar Valon and the White Tower](tar-valon.md) · Members: [WoT Character — Bornhald](../characters/bornhald.md)

The Children of the Light, called Whitecloaks, are a military order sworn to hunt Darkfriends. They see Darkfriends almost everywhere, and they count every woman who channels among them. Chapter 3 gives them one line: a guard says many people believe the Dragon will save them but are "afraid of the Aes Sedai or the Children of the Light". The order enters the story as a name to fear, set beside the Aes Sedai.

- **What they are:** a standing army of zealots, not a church; they answer to no throne.
- **Where:** their fortress is in Amador, and they rule Amadicia in all but name.
- **First seen in the story (Chapter 13):** in Baerlon, arrived the day Moiraine and Lan left south. They claim to be hunting the evil the false Dragon stirs up. The Governor lets only about ten inside the walls; the rest camp north of town. They stalk the streets in white cloaks, order people to "Walk in the Light", nearly come to blows with wagoneers, miners and the Watch, and the Dragon's Fang has appeared on some doors. The gatekeeper Avin spits at their name. In Chapter 14 they claim an Aes Sedai hides in Baerlon. In Chapter 15 three of them walk the streets in burnished breastplates, the crowd parting around them; their young leader, Lord Bornhald, with a golden knot under his sunburst, calls Rand a Darkfriend after Mat's prank splashes them: "We will meet again." One shouts that Baerlon "stands in the Shadow" when the Watch arrives.
- **What they fight:** Darkfriends, Shadowspawn, false Dragons, and anyone who channels, whom they call witches.
- **Why it matters:** sincere hatred of the Shadow, turned into cruelty, makes them dangerous to the heroes even though they are not evil.

## Beliefs, structure and symbols

The order was founded about a thousand years before the story by Lothair Mantelar, as a preaching movement against Darkfriends; it armed itself and never disarmed. Its creed is simple: the Light is good, the Shadow is everywhere, and the One Power is the Shadow's tool.

| Body or symbol | What it is | Who embodies it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lord Captain Commander | Supreme head of the order | Pedron Niall, then Eamon Valda, then Galad |
| Lords Captain and officers | Field commanders; gold knots of rank under the sunburst | Geofram Bornhald |
| The Hand of the Light (Questioners) | An order within the order that extracts confessions by torture; answers to its High Inquisitor | Rhadam Asunawa |
| The white cloak and golden sunburst | Worn by every Child; a Questioner adds a red shepherd's crook | All |
| "Witches" | Their name for Aes Sedai and every woman who channels | — |

**The Two Rivers' view.** In Chapter 3 the villagers know the Children only as a name, feared like the Aes Sedai. Both orders claim to stand against the Shadow, and both frighten ordinary people more than the Shadow does.

The order's path runs from enemy to judge to ally, and most of it passes through Perrin.

## Craft lessons

1. **Make an antagonist sincere.** The Children truly hate the Shadow, which makes them harder to dismiss and more tragic than villains.
2. **Name a power before you show it.** Chapter 3 plants the name in a single fearful line, long before a white cloak appears.
3. **Personify the group.** Bornhald, Byar, Niall, Valda, Asunawa and Galad each show a different face of the same creed.
4. **Let an institution change through one person.** Galad's command shows the order can be redeemed without changing its name.

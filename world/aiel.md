# WoT — Aiel

Oct 16, 2026 · @OncleMarcel

Up: [WoT — World Index](index.md) · Blood: [WoT Character — Rand](../characters/rand.md) · Their war: [WoT Character — Tam](../characters/tam.md) · Chapter cards: [EotW Ch. 4 — The Gleeman](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch04-the-gleeman.md) · [EotW Ch. 6 — The Westwood](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch06-the-westwood.md)

The Aiel are a desert warrior people, and the first six chapters hide them inside Rand. They are never named as his people; they show up as an insult, a joke about his height, and an enemy in Tam's fever. The reader who adds the clues up has Rand's birth before he does.

- **The clues so far.** Rand is a head taller than anyone, with grey eyes and reddish hair (Ch. 1); "Stop acting like a black-veiled Aiel!" and Thom's "as tall as an Aielman" (Ch. 4); "they" who came over the Dragonwall, who "make no peace with strangers" and whose women fight beside the men, and a baby born to one of those women on a mountain slope (Ch. 6); Rand's tired joke that he might meet "a wild, black-veiled Aielman" (Ch. 6).
- **Who they are.** Clans of the Aiel Waste, east of the Spine of the World (the Dragonwall), bound by a code of honour and obligation, ji'e'toh. They veil their faces before they kill.
- **Their women.** Maidens of the Spear fight beside the men; Wise Ones guide the clans, and some channel.
- **The war.** About twenty years before the story they crossed the Dragonwall to punish King Laman of Cairhien for cutting down Avendoraldera, a gift of peace. See the Aiel War card.
- **The prophecy.** They wait for He Who Comes With the Dawn, who will unite them and, they learn, destroy them. He is Rand.

## The Aiel across the series

The Aiel move from a story told about them to the people Rand leads, and then to a people facing their own end. Each step is also a step in Rand learning who he is.

| Book | Where the Aiel stand | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | An insult, a joke about height, Tam's old enemy; a dying Maiden's warning reaches the heroes through the Tinkers | Known only through others' stories |
| 2–3 | Aiel are seen outside the Waste; Gaul joins Perrin; Aiel come to the Stone of Tear | Real people with their own reasons for coming |
| 4 | Rand goes to Rhuidean, sees his ancestors, and is named He Who Comes With the Dawn | Rand learns his birth people; the Aiel learn their history and their fate |
| 5–6 | The clans follow Rand across the Dragonwall; the Shaido refuse him; Aiel fight at Dumai's Wells | His first army, and the first split in his following |
| 7–11 | The Shaido spread through the wetlands and take Faile captive | The code of honour shown at its worst, against Perrin's thread |
| 12–14 | The Aiel face the prophecy that Rand will destroy them; they fight in the Last Battle | A people who must find a new purpose once their reason for being ends |

**The mirror.** The Aiel repeat the Two Rivers' split of authority between women and men: Wise Ones and clan chiefs, as the Women's Circle and the Village Council.

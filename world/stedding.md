# WoT — Stedding

Oct 12, 2026 · @OncleMarcel

Up: [WoT — World Index](index.md)

A stedding is an Ogier haven where no one can touch the One Power. That makes it a refuge, a prison, or a relief, depending on who enters.

## History

- **The Breaking.** Stedding sheltered people from a world torn apart by mad male channelers. Because the Power cannot be touched inside, they were among the few safe places.
- **The Ways.** The Ogier received the Ways as a gift and grew them from their stedding groves. The Ways have since been tainted and are now haunted.
- **Builders.** Ogier stonemasons built many great cities, which ties the stedding to the human world they rarely enter.
- **The Longing.** Ogier who stay away from a stedding too long weaken and die, so every journey out has a cost.

## Atmosphere

A stedding is slow, green and unhurried, with huge trees, singing to wood, and a peace that belongs to another time. Channelers feel a hollow absence the moment they cross the border, as if one of their senses has gone. Everyone else feels calm and safe.

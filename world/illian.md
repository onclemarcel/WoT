# WoT — Illian

Oct 12, 2026 · @OncleMarcel

Up: [WoT — World Index](index.md) · Chapter cards: [EotW Ch. 3 — The Peddler](../chapters/b01-eye-of-the-world/ch03-the-peddler.md)

Illian is a rich port nation on the Sea of Storms, far to the south of the Two Rivers. Chapter 3 drops its name into the peddler's war news, alongside a siege, and for the villagers it is only a word from stories.

- **Government:** a King, checked by a Council of Nine and an Assembly.
- **Old rivalry:** Illian and Tear have hated each other for centuries; their wars are the background of the south.
- **Why it matters now:** each name in Fain's news is a nation the boys will later walk into; Illian becomes one Rand rules.

## Illian across the series

Illian moves from a name in the news to a Forsaken's stronghold, then to a crown Rand takes.

| Books | What happens in Illian | Who is touched |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2–3 | The Great Hunt for the Horn is proclaimed there; hunters swear their oaths in Illian | Mat, the Hunters |
| 4–6 | Sammael rules it in secret as Lord Brend, the power behind the throne | Rand |
| 7 | Rand conquers Illian and is crowned with the Crown of Swords | Rand |
| 8 | Illian is the base for Rand's campaign against the Seanchan | Rand, the Asha'man |

The pattern is a lesson in scale: the first mention is a single word from a peddler, and the same place carries a whole book's climax six volumes later.
